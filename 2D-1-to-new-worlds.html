To New Worlds is a short animation about 2 puppies who ​accidentally travel through a portal. In order to get home they must travel through different worlds in order to return.

To New Worlds was a story that I made up when I was little kid. Instead of my dad telling me bedtime stories, I created this story as a bedtime story often falling asleep before the puppies could return home.

This work was my first animation, drawn and animated using Procreate and edited in Adobe Premiere Pro. Created in Art 75.

This is my second version of To New Worlds. I created and added new scenes, sound and created a longer animation that explores the story's core parts.
