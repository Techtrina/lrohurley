Bio

Lyza Rose O'Hurley is a student at San Jose State University majoring in Digital Media Art and minoring in Geology. She was born in Southern China, adopted at the age of 1 and moved to CA, USA. She has lived in three different states and since entering middle school has lived in SoCal. She uses her life experiences and interests as inspiration for her art. She has experience in many different mediums but is most comfortable with traditional and digital illustration. However she has a strong interest in Blender and gaining 3D modeling as a skill. In her free time she enjoys playing video games, drawing, watching film/anime and writing. Her dream project is to one day adapt the 3 part book series she is currently working on into an animated tv show.
