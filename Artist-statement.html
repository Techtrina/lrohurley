Artist Statement

Portfolio

         Art is a big part of my life and has allowed me to explore new interests, understand myself and better express how I feel. One of my very first introductions and experiences with art was through a game called Animal Jam in 2015. Animal Jam is a free to play virtual and educational game about animals, with multiple features including a painting program. This game was where I discovered drawing and digital illustration. Being centered around animals, this game developed my interest and fascination with the natural world. Not only did I learn more about animals but I started practicing with digital tools, drawing animals and exploring their world. 

          This love for animals eventually led me to another huge part of my childhood. Pokemon heavily influenced me and became the building blocks for creating art. I was introduced to Pokemon around the age of 10, falling in love with it, leading me to watch every show and movie with my dad. This eventually led me to traditional art. While late for school, I was sitting at my desk with my Pokemon Deluxe Essential Handbook opened to #0151, Mew. I don’t remember how I got the idea but I picked up my pencil and started drawing Mew. After that day I continued to draw Pokemon, eventually drawing over 100 of them. Pokemon sparked my imagination and the ability that I could create new things through my art. Eventually I started creating my own Pokemon teams, adventures, even inventing new technology and my own characters. As I continued to draw more and more, I started drawing from my other interests, and realized my love for making art.

          As my creativity grew, I started taking an interest in exploring other mediums I could create with. My curiosity and experimentation with new mediums continued to grow, eventually leading me to mediums very different from traditional ones. I have practiced art ranging from charcoal and painting, digital work, such as 3D modeling and coding, and three-dimensional mediums such as ceramics and metal working. Each of these mediums create form in a different way allowing me to learn how to create art through various ways of thinking and visualization. While I have explored a variety of mediums, illustration has remained my most practiced and most comfortable medium. At the same time I have a strong passion and interest for better learning and understanding 3D modeling to improve my skills and growth as an artist. I realized this passion when I applied to San Jose State University. I originally applied with Animation as my first and Digital Media Art as my second choice. However, not being accepted into the Animation program I was admitted with a major in DMA. At first I was disappointed as my love for illustration would not be my main focus. But after experiencing my first DMA class being Art 75, I realized how expansive art could be and how I can continue to grow. 

          I have always known I wanted to pursue a career in either the film/animation industry or the gaming industry. Film has always been a big part and inspiration in my life, teaching me life lessons, exploring new worlds, and learning how to create a complex story with the many different intricate parts that make up the work. Some of my biggest inspirations came from films and tv shows such as The Cat From Outer Space, Balto 1995 and Epic, as well Phineas and Ferb, Ninjago: Masters of Spinjitzu and Cardcaptor Sakura 1998. Each of these works explore simple ideas through adventurous imagination and creative freedom. They demonstrate perseverance and personal growth through weakness and willingness to keep trying. Through world building they create detailed expression through visuals, sound and storytelling. They have shown me how film and animation create unfamiliar perspectives, inspire various emotions and communicate ideas through the characters and their worlds. 

          Along with this, video games have inspired and influenced how I think about these creative processes and how to better express and communicate myself through my work. KARMA: The Dark World is one of the most influential games I have experienced and has greatly impacted my passion for complex narratives, character development, cinematography and visual storytelling in my own artistic process. KARMA: The Dark World is a psychological thriller set in a dystopian world in 1984 East Germany. This game illustrates the depth of visual and audio immersion through unsettling atmosphere and meaningful yet disturbing experiences. These aspects have heavily influenced how I think about my work and how I want to better express my work with complex aspects yet accessible experience and understanding. 

          Another game that has inspired me greatly is Black Myth: Wukong, an action role playing game rooted in Chinese mythology, exploring one of the most well known narratives, the Journey To The West. Aside from the game's detailed environments, character designs and mechanics, the game draws heavily from Chinese mythology, traditional architecture and historical sites. In fact this game is known for being one of the most architecturally accurate depictions of Chinese culture and heritage. This game is a reflection for my appreciation of world building, visual fidelity and combat animation and movement. What inspires me most is the extensive research, time and craftsmanship invested in the creation of this game. Demonstrating how research is part of the creative process and becomes an integral part of the work. 
          
           My art is not just something I want to create or form but I want my art to have depth and meaning, communicating my own self through the work. Each of these works both in film and gaming have given me experiences and inspirations that have allowed me to grow both as a person and artist. Digital Media Art is the combination of each of these skills, allowing me to create imaginative, complex, and meaningful works. Through illustration and 3D modeling I can continue to grow, developing my skills and furthering my interests to create artwork that becomes a meaningful experience. 

          While I have a strong love for film and video games, recently I have realized just how strong my passion and love for animals is. For my whole life I have lived with pets, including both cats and dogs which sparked my love for learning more about the natural world. As a kid I was constantly consuming media that involved animals whether it was fictional like the Wild Kratts or it was a documentary such as Dinosaur Revolution. This was one of the reasons I was led to getting a minor in Geology. Paleontology is something that has always fascinated me and I never really considered it more than a hobby. But after having taken an Archeology class and the Age of Dinosaurs class, I realized that my interest and love for animals or more specifically prehistoric animals could be more. 

          As an artist, I at first, wasn’t sure how I could apply my skills to my love for prehistoric animals. That is until my dad introduced me to a documentary by Apple TV called Prehistoric Planet. This documentary may seem like any other documentary about dinosaurs. However, the photorealistic CGI and modern paleontological research simulate prehistoric animal behaviors. Along with its cinematography this documentary creates not just a learning experience but an immersive informative film. When I first watched this documentary I was blown away by the realistic visuals and cinematography in which it was shot as if you were filming in the field. Combining my artist skills with my love for prehistoric life I could one day work on a project such as Prehistoric Planet. Creating references for what prehistoric animals looked like, understanding and creating the movement of dinosaurs as well as working on a film or game to further demonstrate how we continue to learn more about the natural world. 

          Each of my inspirations and interests have impacted and influenced my artwork and how I create art. Much of my art is inspired by these interests along with my own experiences and outlook on life. From these experiences I created a work that has been a continuous project since the beginning of high school. My project started out as a simple story with a barely there plot. It became one of my main outlets of creativity and an escape from reality whilst during COVID-19. My initial idea for this project was small, only writing bits and pieces so that I wouldn’t forget the details of my imagination. But as I continued to develop my characters and the story itself, I realized that this project had become a reflection of myself, my experiences and how I understand and communicate with the world around me. This is where it became my dream project and I realized that I wanted to share these ideas with others who could relate to my own experiences. The idea and inspiration to adapt this written series came when I discovered an artist named E_ve. E_ve is a Japanese singer songwriter and vocaloid producer, known for his music featured in anime. What inspired me to adapt my series into an animated tv show, was E_ve’s music videos with visually stunning animation and lyrics that explore experiences of what it means to be. My dream project has become a life long project that expresses me as a person. One day I hope to share this story and the experiences that have shaped who I am. 

          Creating art has led me on a journey, experiencing many new things, learning more about myself and what interests I want to explore. I create art using my inspirations and personal experiences to create work with expression and meaning. Not only is my art an expression of who I am, but it has allowed me to better communicate with the world around me. As I continue to grow, I strive to create art work that others can experience, learn from or relate to and spark someone else' s imagination to create.
