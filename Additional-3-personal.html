These are some of my personal works

This work depicts a Dungeon & Dragons campaign, featuring one of the many different regions of the world. This illustration is a reference to one of my friends character's street racing cars, set in the character's garage.

This work was created in Procreate.

These two works are reference art for my 3 part book series. 

This three-part fantasy saga follows two protagonists on separate journeys through a shared magical world, spanning from a war torn realm to a modernized society, these characters explore what it means to choose darkness as protection, violence as control and self sacrifice as love in order to protect those they love most. 

The work on the left was created using ink and marker.
The work on the right was created using acrylic paint on canvas.
